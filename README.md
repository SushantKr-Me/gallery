5| ## ✨ Key Features
6| 
7| * **Scroll-Driven 3D Architecture:** Smoothly transition through a realistic, depth-receding 3D art gallery powered by **Three.js** and **GSAP ScrollTrigger**.
8| * **Cinematic Entrance:** Enter the gallery by zooming straight through the typography of the **SUSHANT** hero mask.
9| * **Omarchy Palettes & Dynamic Themes:** Seamlessly crossfade between 5 distinct colorways (**Vantablack**, **Nord**, **Dracula**, **Ember**, and **Gallery White**). 
10| * **Interactive 3D Kawaii Cat Companion:** A chibi kitten sits beside the title, tracking your cursor, blinking, twitching her ears, and letting out happy expressions. **Click her** to spin, hop, [...] 
11| * **Ambient Vinyl Player:** Click the spinning vinyl record in the top-right corner to play/pause ambient background audio complete with realistic turntable mechanics.
12| * **Adaptive Performance & High Frame Rate:** Features an adaptive pixel-ratio watchdog to guarantee buttery-smooth rendering across high-end desktop displays and mobile devices alike.

16| ## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries
17| 
18| * **Core 3D & Graphics:** [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) (WebGL Renderer)
19| * **Animation & Timelines:** [GSAP (GreenSock)](https://gsap.com/) & ScrollTrigger
20| * **Smooth Scrolling:** [Lenis](https://lenis.studiofreight.com/)
