# SUSHANT — The Collection

An immersive, scroll-driven 3D web gallery showcasing a 20-work collection of nature photography. Built entirely through modern AI-assisted "vibecoding," this project blends high-end architectural aesthetics with playful interactive elements and smooth hardware-accelerated WebGL performance.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Scroll-Driven 3D Architecture:** Smoothly transition through a realistic, depth-receding 3D art gallery powered by **Three.js** and **GSAP ScrollTrigger**.
* **Cinematic Entrance:** Enter the gallery by zooming straight through the typography of the **SUSHANT** hero mask.
* **Omarchy Palettes & Dynamic Themes:** Seamlessly crossfade between 5 distinct colorways (**Vantablack**, **Nord**, **Dracula**, **Ember**, and **Gallery White**). 
* **Interactive 3D Kawaii Cat Companion:** A chibi kitten sits beside the title, tracking your cursor, blinking, twitching her ears, and letting out happy expressions. **Click her** to spin, hop, trigger star bursts, and cycle through the gallery themes!
* **Ambient Vinyl Player:** Click the spinning vinyl record in the top-right corner to play/pause ambient background audio complete with realistic turntable mechanics.
* **Adaptive Performance & High Frame Rate:** Features an adaptive pixel-ratio watchdog to guarantee buttery-smooth rendering across high-end desktop displays and mobile devices alike.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries

* **Core 3D & Graphics:** [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) (WebGL Renderer)
* **Animation & Timelines:** [GSAP (GreenSock)](https://greens gsap.com/) & ScrollTrigger
* **Smooth Scrolling:** [Lenis](https://lenis.studiofreight.com/)
* **Typography:** [Cormorant Garamond](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond) & [Space Grotesk](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk)
* **Styling & Assets:** Vanilla CSS3, SVG, HTML5 Canvas
